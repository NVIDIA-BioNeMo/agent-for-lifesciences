Design a small protein from scratch, and tell me which parts of it the sequence
design is least sure about.

Generate a backbone of exactly 120 residues. There is no starting structure —
this one comes from nothing. Then design 16 candidate sequences for that
backbone, with no cysteines anywhere in them.

Alongside the sequences you will get a per-position amino-acid probability
matrix. Use it to find the five residue positions where the design was least
confident, and report those positions together with the score of the best
sequence. Say which way the score runs, so I know whether a higher or a lower
number is the better design.
